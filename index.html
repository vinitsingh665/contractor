About ShandyMan

Where Craftsmanship Meets Community

"Every day is a blessed day!"

ShandyMan Contracting Inc. is a certified Canadian Minority-Owned General Contracting company specializing in Renovations, Additions and New Builds across the Greater Toronto Area.

Our mission, vision and core values are the guide to ensuring we deliver results that maximize your return on investment. We exist to ensure that our success benefits the broader community.

With a vision of designing and creating works of art with top talent for home and business owners, we bring the passion, precision, and people-first approach that makes every project a showpiece.

Shane Shand
Founder & President
